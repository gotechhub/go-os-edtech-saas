# 21st.dev tasarım brief'i · Respongo OS

Bu metin yalnızca **sentetik verili tasarım keşfi** içindir; gerçek müşteri adı, kişi, şirket içeriği, parola veya anahtar gönderilmez. 21st bir modelin eğitimi değil, arama/keşif/önizleme aracıdır.

> Design a premium, accessible enterprise learning operating system called Respongo OS. Five SaaS products: GOLMS (administrative learning/compliance), GOLXP (personalized discovery and skills), GOPM (goals/reviews/development), GOCATALOG (licensed learning content), GOAUTHOR AI (AI-assisted authoring). GOFACTORY is a managed production-service portal; GOAI is a shared intelligence layer. Keep three management planes visibly and functionally separate: Respongo OS Core for privileged technical operations, Respongo Super Admin for customer/portal/licence/support operations, and customer Control Center for tenant administration. Create distinct dashboards for learner, tenant admin, instructor, line manager, Super Admin and OS Core operator. The learner view is visual and motivating; management views prioritize tasks, readable dense data and clear actions. Use bold legible sans-serif typography with Turkish glyph support, original restrained brand color, clean SVG icons, generous layout, light/dark themes, responsive 360–1920px, WCAG 2.2 AA interaction, reduced motion, transparent data freshness, meaningful empty/error states. Login: full-screen split layout with form left and a high-quality image right; mobile prioritizes the form. Avoid legacy LMS chrome, fake metrics, generic gradients, needless glass effects and copied competitor branding.

> Design OS Core language-package publication separately from Super Admin language licensing and customer Control Center Language Marketplace/Labels Studio. Turkish is the default interface; Turkish and English are included; eight other languages are licensed add-ons. Show a simple package-version and tenant-override comparison: new platform labels arrive automatically while customer custom text remains. Include an Arabic RTL variant and longer German/Japanese labels. Use only synthetic tenant names and text; no real customer data.

**Çalışma düzeni:** üç anlamlı yön keşfet → öğrenen, admin ve HQ kritik ekranlarında görev testiyle puanla → token/ikon/spacing/motion sistemi seç → 21st katalog kodunu lisans/bağımlılık denetiminden geçir → kendi erişilebilir bileşen paketimize adapte et → web/native tutarlılık kontrolü. 21st bileşeni ürüne doğrudan yapıştırılmaz; iş mantığı ve yetki kodu bizde kalır. Kamuya yayın yapılmaz.

**Kabul:** 5 rol × açık/koyu × 360/390/768/1280/1440/1920; klavye, odak, %200 zoom, reduced motion; kayıtlı tasarım kararları; beş temsilî görev UAT; gerçek veri ve rol durumlarıyla üretim uygulanışı. Tasarım görüntüsü tek başına tamamlanma kanıtı değildir.
